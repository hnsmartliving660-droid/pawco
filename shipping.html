Shipping policy

Free shipping on every order, with estimated delivery times and tracking once your fountain is on its way.

Where we ship

We ship Paw & Co products to the United States and to international destinations, including the European Union.

Shipping costs

Shipping is free on all US orders. Shipping is also free on international and EU orders.

Processing time

Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays) after we receive your order and payment.

Delivery estimates

The times below are estimates, not guarantees. Actual delivery can vary because of the carrier, customs (for some international orders), weather, peak periods, or other events outside our control.

  • United States: estimated 5–10 business days after your order has been processed.
  • International and EU orders: estimated 7–14 business days after your order has been processed.

Cat toys (Tail Chaser Ball, Spinning Feather Toy) ship separately from the Flow Fountain, from our partner warehouse in China, in their own package with free shipping. They usually arrive in about 7–12 business days after processing.

How orders are fulfilled

Orders are packed and shipped by our fulfillment partners. The Flow Fountain ships from warehouses in the United States and the European Union; cat toys ship from a partner warehouse in China. We use the warehouse that best serves your delivery address and available stock.

Tracking

When your order ships, we email tracking information to the address you gave at checkout, whenever the carrier provides a tracking number. Please also check your spam or promotions folder.

Lost or damaged packages

If your package is lost, or it arrives damaged, contact us within 7 days of the estimated or actual delivery date at hnsmartliving660@gmail.com. Please include:

  • Your order number
  • Your shipping address and the tracking number, if you have it
  • For damaged items, clear photos of the outer packaging, the inner packing, and the product

We will work with you and the carrier to send a replacement or issue a refund.

Incorrect or incomplete addresses

Please check your shipping address before you place an order. Delays or failed delivery caused by an incorrect or incomplete address you provided are your responsibility. Additional shipping charges may apply if we need to reship the item.

Customs, duties and taxes

Prices on the site are in US dollars (USD). For some international orders, customs duties, taxes or VAT may be charged by the destination country. Unless we state otherwise at checkout, those charges are your responsibility.

Questions

If you have a question about an order, email hnsmartliving660@gmail.com or visit our Contact page. Please include your order number.
