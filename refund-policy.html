Refund policy

You can return a Flow Fountain within 14 days of delivery. This page explains how returns and refunds work.

14-day returns

If the Flow Fountain is not right for you or your cat, you may return it within 14 days of delivery. The 14-day period starts on the day you receive the order.

Return conditions

  • The item must be unused or in its original condition, with all parts and accessories included, and the original packaging reasonably intact.
  • Contact us by email first so we can send return instructions and a return address. Please do not ship a return until you have heard from us.

How to start a return

Email hnsmartliving660@gmail.com with your order number and the reason for the return. We will reply with instructions. Once we confirm, you can send the item back using a trackable service and keep the proof of postage.

Return shipping

You are responsible for the cost of return shipping, unless the item is defective, arrived damaged, or we sent the wrong item. In those cases, return shipping is free — we will provide a prepaid label or reimburse reasonable return postage.

Refunds

After we receive your return and inspect it, we will refund the purchase price to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Original outbound shipping is free, so there is typically no shipping charge to refund on the original order. Return shipping that you paid — when the return is not for a defect, damage on arrival, or our error — is not refunded.

Defective or damaged on arrival

If the fountain arrives defective or damaged, contact us as soon as you can, and within 14 days of delivery, at hnsmartliving660@gmail.com. Include your order number and a photo or short video of the issue. We will offer a free replacement or a full refund, and we will cover return shipping.

EU right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the European Union or the European Economic Area, you also keep your statutory 14-day right of withdrawal, counted from the day you receive the goods. This policy is in addition to those rights and does not limit them. Any information required under EU consumer law will be provided when you place an order.

Items that cannot be returned

Replacement filters or filter sponges cannot be returned once opened, for hygiene reasons. Unopened replacement filters in their original packaging may be returned under the same 14-day rules.

Questions about a return or refund? Email hnsmartliving660@gmail.com or see our Contact page.
