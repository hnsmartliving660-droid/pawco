Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit this website or place an order, how we use it, and the rights you have.

Data we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details: name, email address, shipping address, and phone number
  • Order information: the product and finish you ordered, quantity, amount paid, order date, and order status
  • Messages you send us by email, phone, or through this website
  • Technical and usage data: such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, through basic cookies and analytics if they are in use

We only collect what we need to take and fulfill orders, provide support, meet legal duties, and operate the site.

Payments

Payments are processed by a third-party payment processor (for example, Stripe). We never store your full card number or security code. The processor collects payment details under its own privacy policy.

How we use your data

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your order
  • To send order, shipping, and tracking emails
  • To handle returns, refunds, and customer support
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations
  • To keep the website secure and, if we use analytics, to understand how the site is used in aggregate

We will not send marketing emails unless you opt in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Under the GDPR, we rely on one or more of these legal bases: performance of a contract (your order), our legitimate interests in running and protecting the shop, a legal obligation, and consent where we ask for it.

Who we share data with

We share personal data only as needed to run the shop:

  • Fulfillment partners, so they can pack and ship your order
  • Carriers, so they can deliver the package and provide tracking
  • Our payment processor, to take payment and issue refunds
  • Professional advisers or public authorities if required by law

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies and analytics

The site may use basic essential cookies needed for it to work, and may use basic analytics to understand traffic. You can control cookies in your browser settings. If we use non-essential analytics, we will do so in a way that respects applicable consent rules.

How long we keep data

We keep order and customer records for as long as needed to fulfill the order, handle returns, and meet tax and legal record-keeping requirements. We then delete or anonymize the data when it is no longer needed.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to certain processing, to receive a copy of your data (portability), and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish data protection authority (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, AEPD) or with your local supervisory authority.

Your rights under the CCPA

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit. You will not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

How to make a request

Email privacy requests to hnsmartliving660@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

Children

This shop is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 (or under 13 in the United States).

International transfers

We are based in Spain. Your data may be processed in Spain, elsewhere in the European Union, and in the United States (for example, by a fulfillment partner or payment processor). Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. The updated policy applies from the date it is posted.
